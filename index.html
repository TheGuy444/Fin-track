Plan for all cards together

Avalanche vs. snowball

Pick one monthly amount for all your cards. Every card gets its minimum, and everything left over goes to one target card. When that card is paid off, its money rolls to the next one.

Strategy
Avalanche usually costs the least interest. Snowball clears whole cards sooner, which can keep you motivated.

How the estimates work

  • Monthly interest is approximated as APR ÷ 12. Real issuers usually use average daily balance and may apply fees, grace periods, promotional APRs and payment-allocation rules.
  • Payoff estimates assume no new purchases, fees or rate changes. If a payment doesn't exceed the monthly interest, the balance never goes down and the tracker says so.
  • Minimum payments are entered by you (or estimated as the larger of $25 or 1% of the balance plus interest); issuers recalculate them each statement.
  • The payoff plan pays every minimum first, then puts the rest toward the highest-APR card (avalanche) or the smallest balance (snowball), rolling freed-up money forward.
  • These are estimates, not financial advice. Check them against your statements.

Back up your data

Copy this save code and paste it into a notepad file (for example fintrack-save.txt). Next time, open this page, click Load, and paste it back. Save again whenever you make changes, since the code is a snapshot.

With a passphrase the code is encrypted (AES-256) and unreadable without it. A forgotten passphrase can't be recovered.
